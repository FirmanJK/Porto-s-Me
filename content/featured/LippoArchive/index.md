A comprehensive digital archive management system developed for the Marketing and Communications Department at Malang Town Square, PT Lippo Group. This system revolutionized document management by transforming a manual paper-based process into a fully digital solution. Features include secure online storage of physical/offline documents, advanced filtering and search capabilities, automated invoice numbering, calendar-based expiration management for large invoices, and complete budgeting features with automated expense monitoring. The system significantly reduced document loss and accelerated archive retrieval times for the entire department.
