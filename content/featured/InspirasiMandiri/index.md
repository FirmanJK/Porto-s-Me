An interactive Progressive Web App (PWA) designed as an educational platform for children. Pintara Kids provides a comprehensive learning ecosystem featuring an interactive story library, thematic worksheets for cognitive development, educational flashcards, and an audio library. The platform creates a safe, engaging, and accessible learning environment that supports both children's education and parents' involvement in their learning journey.
