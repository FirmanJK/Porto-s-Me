A comprehensive web and mobile-based community governance solution designed for local neighborhood management (RT/RW). Jawara provides essential tools for financial management, administrative tasks, and features an integrated local marketplace. The platform enables residents to manage community finances, coordinate activities, and facilitate local commerce, all within a secure and user-friendly digital environment that enhances accessibility for all community members.
