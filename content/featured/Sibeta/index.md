A centralized digital repository and clearance portal designed to facilitate the graduation administrative process for students at Politeknik Negeri Malang. Sibeta simplifies the submission and verification of mandatory graduation documents, including financial clearance (SKLA), library clearance, and alumni data verification. By digitizing these administrative requirements, the platform provides a transparent, efficient, and user-friendly experience for both students and university administrators in tracking and completing their final academic obligations.
