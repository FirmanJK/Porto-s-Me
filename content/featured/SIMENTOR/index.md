SIMENTOR (Sistem Informasi dan Pusat Layanan Mentoring) is a comprehensive digital platform developed for Politeknik Negeri Malang to facilitate and streamline mentoring programs. Serving approximately 45,000 users with remarkable efficiency, the system achieved a 70% improvement in data management with just 10 administrators. Features include automated bulk data import capabilities that can process thousands of student records with a single click, integrated mentoring management system, consultation scheduling, and mentoring progress tracking. The platform connects students with experienced mentors, creating a seamless educational support ecosystem.
