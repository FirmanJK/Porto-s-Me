A professional portfolio website created for UKM Kerohanian Islam (RISPOL) at Politeknik Negeri Malang. This platform serves as the digital face of the Islamic Spirituality organization, showcasing comprehensive organizational profiles, detailed member biographies, program introductions, and extensive information about the organization's activities and initiatives. The website features a modern, responsive design that effectively communicates the organization's mission, vision, and values while providing an intuitive user experience for visitors seeking information about Islamic spiritual activities at the campus.
