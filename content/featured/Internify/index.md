An integrated web-based internship management system specifically developed for Politeknik Negeri Malang to streamline the entire internship lifecycle. The platform assists students in discovering placements that align with their personal interests, technical competencies, and academic profiles. Utilizing the Grey Relational Analysis (GRA) method for intelligent matching, Internify provides a comprehensive ecosystem for students, supervisors, and administrators to manage internship applications, daily monitoring, performance evaluations, and final reporting in one unified dashboard.
